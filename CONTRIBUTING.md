# Contributing

Keep observations, interpretations, and implementation behavior separate.
Record sources, reproduction steps, limitations, and the rights in each input.
Follow the [draft shared research policy](https://github.com/OpenJTD/spec/blob/main/RESEARCH-POLICY.md).

Open a focused pull request against `main`. Sign off authored commits with
`git commit -s` under the [Developer Certificate of Origin 1.1](https://developercertificate.org/).
Apache-2.0 applies to contributions we author; externally obtained documents
retain their own terms. A sign-off does not replace permission to share a file.

Do not upload confidential or restricted material to public issues or pull
requests. Report implementation vulnerabilities privately to the affected
implementation's maintainers.
